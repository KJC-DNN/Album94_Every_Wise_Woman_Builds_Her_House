# Album 94 — Every Wise Woman Builds Her House

Seven bilingual songs from Proverbs 14:1–16:23. A wise woman builds her house. A foolish one pulls it down with her hands. The house of the wicked is overthrown. A soft answer turns away wrath. Commit your works to the Lord. In the light of the King’s countenance is life. Tamil original plus English cinematic companion.
